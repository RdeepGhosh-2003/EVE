=========================================
          FERRARI - THE PRANCING HORSE
=========================================

1. INTRODUCTION
Ferrari S.p.A. is an Italian luxury sports car manufacturer based in Maranello, Italy. Founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1939 out of the Alfa Romeo race division as Auto Avio Costruzioni, the company built its first car in 1940 and produced its first Ferrari-badged car in 1947.

2. THE FOUNDER: ENZO FERRARI
Enzo Ferrari (1898–1988) was an Italian motor racing driver and entrepreneur. He founded the Scuderia Ferrari Grand Prix motor racing team, and subsequently the Ferrari automobile marque. He was affectionately known as "Il Commendatore" or "Il Drake".

3. THE LOGO (CAVALLINO RAMPANTE)
The famous symbol of Ferrari is a black prancing horse on a yellow shield, usually with the letters "S F" (for Scuderia Ferrari), with three stripes of the Italian national colors (green, white, and red) at the top. The horse was originally the symbol of Count Francesco Baracca, a legendary Italian air force ace of World War I.

4. FORMULA ONE RACING (SCUDERIA FERRARI)
Scuderia Ferrari is the oldest and most successful active Formula One team, having competed in every world championship since the 1950 season. 
- World Constructors' Championships: 16
- World Drivers' Championships: 15
- Legendary Drivers: Michael Schumacher, Niki Lauda, Alberto Ascari, Juan Manuel Fangio, Gilles Villeneuve, Kimi Räikkönen, Fernando Alonso, Sebastian Vettel, and Charles Leclerc.

5. ICONIC ROAD CARS
- Ferrari 250 GTO (1962): One of the most valuable and sought-after classic cars in history.
- Ferrari F40 (1987): The last car personally approved by Enzo Ferrari, celebrating the 40th anniversary.
- Ferrari F50 (1995): A Formula 1 car for the road.
- Enzo Ferrari (2002): Named after the founder, featuring advanced F1 technology.
- LaFerrari (2013): Ferrari's first hybrid supercar.
- SF90 Stradale (2019): A plug-in hybrid supercar.
- Purosangue (2022): Ferrari's first-ever four-door, four-seat vehicle (SUV/FUV).

6. CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS & FACTORY
- Location: Maranello, Emilia-Romagna, Italy.
- Fiorano Circuit: Ferrari's private testing track located near Maranello.
- Mugello Circuit: Also owned by Ferrari.
